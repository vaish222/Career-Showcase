The Problem
Print volumes were declining while fixed operational costs remained high. The organization also faced an upcoming facility transition and needed to determine whether continuing internal print operations was sustainable.
My Role
Led product and technical delivery across discovery, requirements, solution design, engineering coordination, vendor integration, testing, and production rollout.
How I Approached It
Worked across business leaders, operations, engineering, QA, managers, directors, and the external vendor to understand business constraints, technical dependencies, and transition risks. Helped define the requirements and rollout approach while ensuring existing print workflows could continue during migration.
Key Product Decisions
- Balance business continuity with migration speed
- Support existing internal printing until vendor workflows were ready
- Define integration and testing requirements across environments
- Coordinate dependencies across internal teams and Fiserv
Outcome / Impact
Supported an enterprise transformation estimated to deliver ~$1.5M in annual savings while moving the organization from a largely fixed-cost print model toward a variable-cost model.
What I Learned
Large product decisions are rarely purely technical. The right solution has to balance customer needs, operational risk, economics, technical feasibility, and organizational change.
